What the prototype demonstrates

  • Rows stay contiguous. Lead, Bass, Kick and Hat never gain a panel between them. The dock takes its space from the bottom of the scroller and pins there while the tracks scroll above it.
  • One scroller. The ruler, every row and the dock share the horizontal scroll, so step 9 is the same x everywhere. The dock's control panel and keyboard are sticky to the left like the control column.
  • Accessible control block, 154px. Desktop identity and controls have 24px targets, with a 2px gap inside a 58px row. The instrument badge auditions; a single name click opens Track and a double-click renames. Copy/Paste, Clear and Delete remain on their target rows. Landscape uses 44px controls and an expanded editor.
  • One left edge. The grip, the names, the ruler's spacer, the dock toolbar and the keyboard all start on the same line, and the dock grid starts where the step cells start.
  • Row and dock are tied three ways. The docked control block gets a 2px accent edge, its ♪ turns orange, and on open its cells pulse once. The visible dock heading and tab carry the same name and category tick.
  • Switching is a crossfade, not a layout change. Press the other track's ♪ or tab: notes fade over 120ms, ghosts swap, the pitch scroll eases to the new notes. Nothing above moves.
  • Ghost notes answer "two rolls at once". The other melodic track is drawn dashed in its category colour. Toggle it in the Notes pane.
  • Three panes as one toolbar, one grid, two heights. Notes, Step and Track swap the toolbar under the dock header. Step highlights the selected step's column. Track holds transpose, steps, instrument, pattern tools and actions. The Notes grid stays visible for melodic tracks; drums open the Step or Track toolbar. The ⇕ button toggles the compact and tall detents.
  • Pitch contour in the cells. Melodic rows show where each note sits, so the melody stays readable while the dock shows another track.

Try it

  1. Press Play. Playheads run on the ruler, the cells and the dock from one clock.
  2. Press ♪ on Bass and watch the crossfade and the control edge move. Press ♪ on Lead to come back.
  3. Drag across empty dock cells to paint, drag a bar up or down to re-pitch, drag its right edge to lengthen it. The row above updates on the same frame.
  4. Shift+click a Lead cell. The dock switches to the Step pane for that step; change its pitch or tie there.
  5. Open the Track pane and change transpose. Every bar shifts, and the readout in the row follows.
  6. Toggle the tall detent with ⇕. Turn on "Lock to scale" and drop a note on a dimmed row. Press Escape to close the dock.
