Keyboardia · Pitch panel · Editor dock prototype

Piano roll dock

The sequencer with the proposed editor dock under the grid. Each row's control tile puts the track on the left (name over M and S) and the pattern on the right. Press the keyboard icon on Piano, Bass or Duo to open Notes; press the gear, the transpose or the step count to open Track. Double-click a name to rename it; hover it for the sample ID, and the instrument once the track is renamed. Hold or Shift+click a step to open Step. Rows stay contiguous; the dock opens at 18px per semitone and scrolls to the track's notes. Note editing and playback use a small synth; step-count, instrument, envelope and pattern-tool integration are labelled, disabled previews. Copy/Paste, Clear and Delete are working prototype actions in this spec-only mock. Scale selection and reorder are previews; portrait shows a listening placeholder. The dock starts open here so the page shows its working state; in the app it opens only on request. The pattern is the owner's session Piano in A Minor Pentatonic plus a Bass demonstration row with every kind of lock.

What the prototype demonstrates

  • Rows stay contiguous. No panel ever opens between rows. The dock takes its space from the bottom of the scroller and pins there while the tracks scroll above it.
  • One scroller, one lattice. The ruler, every row and the dock share the horizontal scroll and the same 36px columns with 3px gutters, so step 9 is the same x everywhere. Rows are 50px on a 53px pitch.
  • The control tile, 231px. Left, the track: name over [M|S]. Right, the pattern: [Notes|Lane|Tools] over [Copy|Clear|Delete], with [transpose|steps] beside. Every control is visible on 24px targets.
  • One button language. Commands and values are solid chips; state and view selection are hollow boxes that fill when on; information is bare text. The tile, the dock, the panes and the transport all follow it.
  • Icons from production's system. Lucide paths at 16px with a 2px stroke on a 24px grid; Notes and Lane are drawn to the same rules.
  • Row and dock are tied. The docked tile lifts and its name turns accent; its Notes or Tools box fills cyan; the dock's tab for that track is selected and carries the same colour strip.
  • Switching is a crossfade, not a layout change. Open another track's Notes or tab: notes fade over 120ms, ghosts swap, the pitch scroll eases to the new notes. Nothing above moves.
  • Ghost notes answer "two rolls at once". Other melodic tracks are drawn dashed in their category colour. Toggle them in the Notes pane.
  • Three panes as one toolbar, one grid, two heights. Notes, Step and Track swap the toolbar under the dock header. Track holds transpose, the velocity lane, mute and solo, the pattern actions and rename. The Tall and Compact button switches the detents.
  • Marks in the cells. A step's fill rises to its volume; a corner badge gives a pitch lock's amount; a tie is one shape across the gap; a fold marks an envelope lock; a dashed cell keeps a lock on a step that is off.

Try it

  1. Press Play. Playheads run on the ruler, the cells and the dock from one clock.
  2. Press the keyboard icon on Duo and watch the crossfade and the docked state move. Press it on Piano to come back.
  3. Drag across empty dock cells to paint, drag a bar up or down to re-pitch, drag its right edge to lengthen it. The row above updates on the same frame.
  4. Shift+click a Piano cell. The dock switches to the Step pane for that step; change its pitch or tie there.
  5. Press a row's gear to open Track and change transpose. Every bar shifts, and the row's transpose chip follows.
  6. Switch to Tall. Turn on "Lock to scale" and drop a note on a dimmed row. Press Escape to close the dock.
