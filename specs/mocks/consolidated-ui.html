Where each consolidation shows

C1One disclose system. The floating inspector replaces the mobile drawer, the landscape accordion, and the floating p-lock editor. Steps, tracks, slots, names — same surface.
C2One dropdown. "Invite ▾" is now the portal dropdown (was an ad-hoc div); scale, step-count, FX preset — same component, same Escape/✓ behavior.
C3One transport, one FX panel. The mobile TransportBar and EffectsPanel are deleted; these components render responsively everywhere.
C4TrackRow 13 → 6. Grip · name · M · S · LANE · key, on a rigid column grid. Transpose, step-count, ⚙ tools, Copy/Clear/Delete live in the inspector; ♪/▎ became the lane.
C5Sliders 6 → 4. DragLCDs for numerics (BPM, swing, transpose), RangeSlider for continuous params, the property lane, the XY pad. The drag-number div and the ruler are gone.
C6Segmented → chip row. FX | MIX | PITCH is a chip row (one aux panel open at a time) — the same component pattern slots will ride in Arc 4.
C9Pending states unified. "✓ Saved," "Copied ✓," and future cue countdowns share one pill grammar.
C12One category carrier. The name tick — here, in the inspector title, and on every surface below. No edge strips anywhere.
